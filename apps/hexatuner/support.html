HexaTuner™

HexaTuner tunes all six strings of your guitar from a single strum: each string gets its own reading at the same time. You can also tune one string at a time, or play any note in Chromatic mode.

Contact

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the best readings?

Strum the open strings firmly, with the phone close to the guitar, in a quiet room. Tune the low strings first: the higher strings share overtones with the lower ones, and their readings settle once the low strings are in tune. A reading marked ≈ is approximate for that reason.

What are the three modes?

Strum reads all six strings at once. By string is for plucking one string at a time; a string gets a tick once it has held in tune for a second, and you can tap a row to lock onto that string. Chromatic shows any note you play, with a hint against the tuning you've chosen.

The app says my guitar sounds like a different tuning.

If you strum a guitar that's in a different tuning from the one selected (say, standard tuning with the app set to Full step down), HexaTuner says so instead of showing misleading readings. Tap Switch to… to change the app's tuning, or Use By string to retune the guitar string by string.

Which tunings are included?

Standard, Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, Open E, Half step down and Full step down. Choose one from the tuning button at the top of the screen. You can also set the reference pitch (A4) between 415 and 466 Hz there.

It isn't hearing my guitar.

Check that HexaTuner has microphone access in Settings › HexaTuner › Microphone. In the app's settings, set Sensitivity to High. Hold the phone closer to the guitar and strum a little harder.

What do the arrows mean?

Each reading says which way to turn: ↑ tune up when a string is flat, ↓ tune down when it's sharp, and ✓ when it's in tune. When a string is more than 50 cents out, double chevrons at the end of its meter point back toward the center, the way to tune.

Does the screen stay on?

Yes, while HexaTuner is listening. It lets the screen sleep normally when you leave the app.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device and works offline.

Does the app collect any data?

No. Audio is analyzed on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
