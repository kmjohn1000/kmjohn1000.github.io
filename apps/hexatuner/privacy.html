Privacy Policy

HexaTuner™ · Last updated October 12, 2026

HexaTuner is a guitar tuner by Kristopher John. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no network requests from the app to any server.

The microphone

HexaTuner asks for access to your microphone so it can hear your guitar. The sound is analyzed on your device, as it comes in, to measure the pitch of each string. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the app keeps nothing of it once each reading is made.

You can turn microphone access off or on at any time in Settings › HexaTuner › Microphone. Without it, the app can't tune.

What the app stores on your device

Your settings (the tuning, reference pitch, sensitivity, and the last mode you used) are saved on your device so they're there the next time you open the app. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

HexaTuner is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no personal or payment information about you. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first, before that change ships in an update.
